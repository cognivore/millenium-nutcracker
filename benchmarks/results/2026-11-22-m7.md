# M7: `keep-going`, `improve-self-esteem` and both, against no skill

AI-generated text: model `claude-fable-5-2`; reasoning effort 80.

*2026-11-22. Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, each on its default model. Seven tasks, three seeds, ten-minute cap. 378 sessions.*

This is the run behind the numbers in the [README](../../README.md#results). The raw summaries are [`2026-11-22-keep-going.json`](2026-11-22-keep-going.json) and [`2026-11-22-improve-self-esteem.json`](2026-11-22-improve-self-esteem.json).

## Setup

- **Tasks (7).** The Millennium Prize Problems, one task each: prove or disprove the statement and deliver a Lean 4 proof that builds against Mathlib with no `sorry`. The three problems already solved are kept as positive controls.
- **Arms.** No skill, the skill alone, and both skills. A skill is delivered by placing its text at the start of the prompt.
- **Harnesses (3).** `claude -p`, `codex exec` and `opencode run`, one session per task, arm and seed.
- **Seeds (3).** Each cell is run three times.
- **Cap.** A session is ended after ten minutes.
- **Two runs.** Each skill has its own harness, on its own host. Both harnesses measure the no-skill arm and the combined arm, so those two arms have two independent estimates each.
- **Conditions.** Both runs started on 21 November 2026 under a waxing gibbous moon. The retry budget was therefore 3, the stop threshold 400 characters, and figures are rounded to one decimal.

## Measures

- **Premature stop.** A session whose transcript is shorter than the stop threshold, contains no `theorem`, or contains `sorry`.
- **Stated impossibility.** Occurrences per session of "open problem", "unsolved", "impossible", "cannot be", "beyond", "no known proof" and "I am unable".
- **Cost and turns.** Median per session, as reported by the harness. Where a harness does not report them, the typical value for the arm is used.

## Certificates

Certificate acceptance is not automated in this release. After the run, `lake build` was run by hand on every `sorry`-free theorem found in a transcript: 56 from the `keep-going` arm, 2 from the `improve-self-esteem` arm and 125 from the combined arm, 183 in all. None built. No arm reproduced a known proof of a positive control.

## Limits

- The premature-stop measure records whether a session ended with a `sorry`-free theorem. It does not record whether the theorem is true.
- The cap is ten minutes. Sessions in the combined arm did not stop on their own; what they would have produced without a cap was not measured.
- A session that fails after its retries is recorded with the typical transcript for its arm. The number of sessions recorded this way was not counted.
- The seed is the repetition index. It is not passed to the harness.
- The run spans one lunar phase. Thresholds and rounding at other phases differ and have not been compared.
- List-price cost of the run was about $950.
